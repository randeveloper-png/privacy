Stack Up Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the game “Stack Up” (the “App”), published by RANDEV (“we”, “us”), handles information. Contact: randevloper@gmail.com

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, precise location, or contacts. The App saves your best score on your own device only. It is not sent to us.

Advertising

The App shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), ad interactions, and diagnostic information to show and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize ads. Where required by law, you will be asked for your consent before ads are personalized. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. See Google’s policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell your data. Information processed through ads is handled by Google under its own privacy policy and is encrypted in transit.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
